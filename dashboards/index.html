Governance Controls

01

LLM is not authorityPolicy and human approval authorize operational writes.

02

Live Evidence is authoritativeCognia Knowledge and Operational Memory cannot override current production evidence.

03

MCP is the operational boundaryControl Plane agents do not receive native operational provider clients.

04

Cognia is Knowledge onlyGoverned Knowledge is separate from MCP operations and PostgreSQL/pgvector Operational Memory.

05

Approval is bound and one-timeTool, action, target, parameters, runbook and environment are bound before execution; consumption is irreversible.

06

Execution capability is signed and short-livedThe execution boundary receives a scoped capability only after the approval and binding are validated.

07

Verification is independentCommand success is not equivalent to recovery; post-action operational symptoms must be verified before learning.
